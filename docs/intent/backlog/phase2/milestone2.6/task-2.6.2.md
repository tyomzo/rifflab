title: "Drag-to-reorder effects"
status: pending
crate: "rifflab-ui"
requirement: "FR-M7-13"
---

Enable drag-to-reorder of effect nodes in the Effects Editor view. Dragging an effect node to a new position updates the EffectChain order. Provide visual feedback during the drag (insertion indicator, ghost element). The reorder must take effect immediately in the audio processing chain.
