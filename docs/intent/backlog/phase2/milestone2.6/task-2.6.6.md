Add a cue lane to the arrangement timeline view. Display color-coded markers for different cue types: section labels, effect preset switches, and loop regions. Click a marker to edit or delete the cue. Right-click on the lane to add a new cue at that position. Markers should align with the waveform timeline and scroll/zoom with it.
